CONTROLS AND HELP

WASD or Arrow Keys to Move

Space Bar for Blaster

J for missile

K for mines (unlockable)

L for laser (unlockable)

Right shift for shield (unlockable)

GUIDE FOR DUMMIES

This is a game centered around surviving waves of enemies and defeating bosses. Using knowledge gained in your mission debriefs, you can drastically add to your upgrade points by answering quiz questions after defeating a series of enemy waves.

GUIDE FOR EVEN BIGGER DUMMIES

This is a hard game, but doable if you strategically use your weapons and grab health when needed. I would have preferred you get good at the game yourself, but here is the closest thing to an easy mode.

Asteroids drop health consistently, and later on Swarm Type enemies have a small chance of dropping them too.

Use your Blaster for quick damage to bosses and enemies, but poor burst damage.

Use your missile to take out most weaker enemies in a single shot. Does moderate burst damage, tracks enemies but also hits asteroids in the way, so you might now always hit.

Mines are used to defend against melee attackers and contact damage from asteroids and other enemies. These can be creatively used to defend oneself against mobs of enemies that would otherwise kill you quickly.

Shield - Makes you invulnerable for a brief period with a cooldown. Great against weak and fast enemies and asteroids, but may cause unintended bouncing off of enemies. Great against powerful boss attacks and swarms.

Lasers do little damage to bosses but are excellent for destroying asteroids and weak enemies in a pinch. Used for when you need to clear asteroids for health or as an obstacle
